# poap.thingelstad.xyz

A time capsule and tribute to POAP, the Proof of Attendance Protocol (2019 to 2026), told
through Jamie Thingelstad's years with it: as a collector, as an issuer of 130 drops, and
through the network of people who collected them.

This repo is the site. The data lives in
[jthingelstad/poap-archive](https://github.com/jthingelstad/poap-archive): the collection,
the drops, the timeline, the network, Jamie's writing about POAP, and background on the
company. Start with that repo's `BRIEF.md`.

Status: not started. The design is open.
