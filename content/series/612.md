---
title: "612 POAP Challenge"
---

A summer scavenger hunt across Minneapolis in 2025. Jamie made one location drop for each
of 40 landmarks, towers, bridges, parks and venues in the 612 area code, all created on
one day, and anyone who visited could mint the drop for that place. Finishing the set
earned an achievement token. Jamie wrote it up in
[612 POAP Challenge: Collect them all!](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/05/31/poap-challenge-collect-them-now.html),
and [POAP2RSS](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/07/06/vibe-coding-poaprss.html), which
Jamie built, powered its site.
