title: "Jamie on POAP"
---

1. **[POAP Wishlist](https://www.thingelstad.com/2023/01/14/poap-wishlist.html)**
   (2023-01-14). The main product memo: integration hooks (RSS and webhooks), signed and
   location-based events, and wallet-free onboarding. POAP later shipped the onboarding
   and location ideas. RSS never came from POAP, so Jamie built it.
2. **[POAP as a Business](https://www.thingelstad.com/2023/01/16/poap-as-a.html)**
   (2023-01-16), published the same day as
   [Premium Features for POAP Issuers](https://www.thingelstad.com/2023/01/16/premium-features-for.html)
   and [POAP Extensions](https://www.thingelstad.com/2023/01/16/poap-extensions.html). A
   full pricing and packaging proposal: Collector at $3 a month, Pro at $20, Studio at
   $500 an event, Business, and a free Non-Profit tier. Collections, one of the ideas,
   shipped a year later.
3. **[New POAP Onboarding Process](https://www.thingelstad.com/2024/01/14/new-poap-onboarding.html)**
   (2024-01-14). Exactly one year after the wishlist: POAP shipped email reservations
   that prompt the holder to act, and an embedded wallet with an onpoap.eth name.
4. **[Vibe Coding POAP2RSS](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/07/06/vibe-coding-poaprss.html)**
   (2025-07-06), after [POAP + RSS = Awesome?](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/01/05/poap-rss-awesome.html)
   (2025-01-05). Tired of waiting for RSS, Jamie specified it and built poap2rss.com with
   AI coding tools. It went on to power the 612 Challenge and Airport Rally sites.
5. **[612 POAP Challenge: Collect them all!](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/05/31/poap-challenge-collect-them-now.html)**
   (2025-05-31). Once location drops existed, Jamie turned them into a summer scavenger
   hunt around Minneapolis. That led to the
   [Airport POAP Rally Website](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/12/26/airport-poap-rally-website.html)
   (2025-12-26) and the Flight Path globe ([WT342](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/342/)).
